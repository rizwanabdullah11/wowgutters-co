Why Gutters Matter
A gutter carries rain off the roof. If it can’t, water spills behind it and into the fascia, soffit, wall and eventually inside. [How Professional Gutter Cleaning Extends the Life of Your Roofline](https://wowgutters.co.uk/blog/professional-gutter-cleaning-extends-roofline-life) explains the chain, and [Water Damage From Gutters: Birmingham Homeowner Guide](https://wowgutters.co.uk/blog/gutter-water-damage-guide) walks through the stages. [How Clean Gutters Help Protect Your Home Throughout the Year](https://wowgutters.co.uk/blog/how-clean-gutters-protect-your-home-year-round) shows what stays protected when the system works.
The catch is that gutters fail quietly. Silt compacts at the channel base over several seasons, and a partly blocked gutter still works in light rain, so nothing looks wrong until heavy rain exceeds what’s left. [Hidden Gutter Damage Is Easier to Spot in Summer](https://wowgutters.co.uk/blog/hidden-gutter-damage-easier-spot-summer) explains why you can’t see it from below.
Signs Your Gutters Need Attention
Ten minutes on a dry day, from the garden:
A section that sits lower than its neighbours.
A faint vertical stain below the gutter line.
Plants or a green fringe at the edge.
A gurgle from a downpipe in light rain.
A puddle that keeps returning at a downpipe base.
Peeling paint on the fascia.
A musty smell near an outside wall indoors.
Work through the [blocked gutters checklist](https://wowgutters.co.uk/blog/gutters-blocked-checklist) as you go. Signs Birmingham Homes Show](https://wowgutters.co.uk/blog/downpipe-blocked-signs-birmingham-homes) cover them in detail. What a Proper Visit Covers
Ground-level walk-round. Staining, sagging, growth and access.
Extraction to the channel floor. Leaves and the silt beneath them.
Camera inspection. Joints, brackets and fall angle on a live feed.
Downpipe flow test. Every outlet.
Fascia and soffit observation. Visible condition noted and photographed.
Roof surface check. Moss and slipped tiles flagged.
Photos and a written summary. Before and after images of every run.

How to Compare Contractors
Ask five questions.
Do you clear to the channel floor and check the structure? See [The One Question to Ask Before Hiring Any Gutter Cleaner in the West Midlands](https://wowgutters.co.uk/blog/one-question-before-hiring-gutter-cleaner-west-midlands).
Do you work from the ground?
Are you fully insured, and can I see the certificate?
Do I get before and after photos of every run?
Will you tell me what’s outside your scope?
Be cautious about unusually quick visits, as [How Long Does a Professional Gutter Clean Take in Birmingham?](https://wowgutters.co.uk/blog/gutter-clean-duration) explains.
Doorstep Callers
Unrequested callers who claim to have spotted a problem on your roof are worth treating with caution. Most aren’t criminals, but the pattern of an unprompted visit, a claimed fault you can’t check and pressure to decide on the spot is a recognised risk. Don’t agree to anything at the door, get more than one quote and check the business exists. [Age UK](https://www.ageuk.org.uk/) and [Citizens Advice](https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/) have plain guidance.
